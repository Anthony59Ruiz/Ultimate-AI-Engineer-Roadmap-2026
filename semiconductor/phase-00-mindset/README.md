# Phase 0 — Mindset & Orientation

The foundation phase of the
[Semiconductor Supply-Chain AI roadmap](../../roadmaps/semiconductor-supply-chain.md).
No code — the goal is an accurate mental model of the job, the domain, and where
AI creates value, captured in three portfolio artifacts.

## Learning objectives

- Understand the **AI Engineer** role: integrate existing models into products
  (vs. the ML Engineer, who trains models from raw data).
- Map the **semiconductor supply chain** end-to-end and know why it's uniquely
  fragile (concentration, long lead times, allocation, geopolitics).
- Recognize the **four AI value patterns** — Forecasting, Risk/Anomaly,
  Document AI, Copilot/Agent — and where each applies.
- Think in **leading vs. lagging indicators** and **signal vs. noise** to spot
  disruptions before they happen.

## What I learned

- The high-value AI work is **integration and systems thinking**, not training
  models from scratch — powerful models are available cheaply via **APIs**.
- Copilots answer from **connected company data via retrieval (RAG)**, not from
  their training data — a foundational distinction for Phase 8.
- The easiest, fastest AI wins are usually **Document AI**; the highest-value,
  hardest wins are usually **Risk/Forecasting**. Ship easy wins first.
- The 2020–2023 auto chip shortage was **unwatched, not unpredictable** — the
  leading indicators existed; nobody connected them in time. That gap is the job.
